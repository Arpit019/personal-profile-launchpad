## Proof of Work Standards (The Product Space)
### Capstone Project Pattern
- Real company focus (Meesho, Zerodha, Uber, Netflix, HealthifyMe, Canva, Swiggy, Flipkart, Lenskart, Navi)
- Problem-first framing with specific business challenge
- Data-driven approach with metrics and analysis
- Practical solutions with implementation roadmaps

### Testimonial Patterns (What Hiring Managers Value)
- "Built a real feature roadmap and launching a pricing revamp project" → APM at Swiggy
- "Executed entire user journey flow and analytics integration" → PM at HealthifyMe
- "Go-To-Market strategy with visuals and metrics" → Growth Associate at Meesho
- "AI feature prioritization with data, metrics, and impact" → Lead PM at FirstCry
- "User onboarding revamp with thinking, design decisions, and A/B test plans" → PM at Slice

### Key Takeaways for Portfolio Content
1. Every project must have quantified impact (%, $, users, time saved)
2. Process matters: show frameworks used, not just results
3. Visual proof: screenshots, Figma links, metrics dashboards
4. Real companies: demonstrate ability to think about actual products
5. Problem → Process → Solution → Impact structure
6. Trade-off analysis: why this solution over alternatives
7. Data-driven: user research, A/B tests, analytics
